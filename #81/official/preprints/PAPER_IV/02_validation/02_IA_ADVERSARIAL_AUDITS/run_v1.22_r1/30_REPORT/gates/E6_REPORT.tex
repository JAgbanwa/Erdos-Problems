  \begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item

    2-up sheets (\texttt{pairs/}): EN 72 pages and ES 73 pages.

    only from a sheet that is visible to the auditor when the row is
    written. Discarded and unconfirmed intermediate logs are kept for
    transparency.
  \end{itemize}

  The author\textquotesingle s response and
  \texttt{VISUAL\_REVIEW\_v1.22.md} were contrasted after the
  inspection.

matching/packing residues in Spanish prose are translated. In fact, only
the figure label changed; none of the 13 prose occurrences was
translated. The response\textquotesingle s NEW-01 row ("Se restaura el
formato del namespace Model donde corresponde") holds for EN only.
\texttt{VISUAL\_REVIEW\_v1.22.md} reports "valor base" and {[}6{]},
which is correct, and does not claim the prose translation.
\textbf{Severity: MINOR} (presentation; no mathematical content).

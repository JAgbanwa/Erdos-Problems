## Corrections to the proposed exposition

- The recovery corollary assumes rooted defect on G and u≥0. Its Lean interface
  takes rational u. The manuscript distinguishes that interface from the same
  elementary real-parameter argument.
- No core matching restriction is attributed to rooted defect alone. The
  argument uses many common light neighbors and a bound on their clique sizes.
- The interior edit charge is e(S)−choose(|C|,2), bounded by E+rn. The later
  change of host class supplies the second rn; isolation and reinsertion of
  W supply the two nt_W terms.
- Case B2 follows the selected proof: ranks are normalized without changing
  comparisons, then bounded by n. The round count B in Case A is not reused
  as the subdivision bound.
- The proposed notation t=|W| was not used: it would collide with the
  first-phase triangle count.

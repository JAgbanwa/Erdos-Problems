## Supporting implications, not missing assumptions

- E32.cp_classification_of_theoremCPrimeC is a conditional implication. Here its premise is supplied by FixedExplicit.fixed_defect_stability_explicit; do not cite the implication alone as an unconditional classification.
- NormalizedDeficit, ResidualExcess, NormalizedCliqueCore and RootPartition produce the graph data combined by NormalizedStability. Their retained inequalities are summarized in (6.17).
- E32.DeletionInduction supplies the deficit-sensitive induction; Appendix F.1 states its nonconstant induction window.
- E32.PieceIdentity and FixedDefectPieceEdges give partition accounting relative to the defect root. PieceEdgeStability gives the local factor ten.
- ComparatorResize and OptimalCoreDistance control the movement to an optimal-size template.
- RootScore makes the choice of root independent of both the requested accuracy and the partition.
- SublinearEdit, RootedCliqueRecovery and SublinearStabilityTransfer produce a clique in the original graph after a chordal edit. This step is not obtained by merely putting a growing s into fixed-s stability.

## Attributions and nonclaims

The graph class, Q_s, fixed-defect eventual unrestricted maximum and equality family are attributed to Okechukwu [15, Theorem 1.1]. The signed-star estimate follows [15, Lemma 3.1]. E34's removal development follows de Joannis de Verclos [22].

The comparison with [15, Corollary 5.5] uses the optimal-family estimate with its square-root term, not the linear estimate to an arbitrary-size template. The comparison with [15, Theorem 1.3] uses uniform localization and arbitrary-order sequences. No replacement for all results of [15], or historical priority, is claimed.

The local factor ten has a sharp numerical profile (3,2). This does not establish optimality of the global constants 480, A_s or any threshold.
